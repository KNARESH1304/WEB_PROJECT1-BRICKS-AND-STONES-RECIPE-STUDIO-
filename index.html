Margherita Pizza

Prep Time 20 mins
Cook Time 25 mins
Difficulty Easy
Serves

Safety notes

⛔ High risk Oven at 240°C. Mitigation: use oven mitts and a timer, keep children away.

⚠ Medium risk Contains gluten and dairy. Mitigation: check labels if you have allergies.

✔ Low risk Raw dough. Mitigation: wash hands and tools after handling.

Ingredients

  • 125g pizza dough
  • 50ml tomato sauce
  • 75g fresh mozzarella
  • 250g pizza dough
  • 100ml tomato sauce
  • 150g fresh mozzarella
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 200ml tomato sauce
  • 300g fresh mozzarella
  • 750g pizza dough
  • 300ml tomato sauce
  • 450g fresh mozzarella
  • 10 fresh basil leaves
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp salt

Instructions

1

Prepare the Dough

⛔ High risk Hot oven or hot tray. Use mitts.

Preheat oven to 240°C (465°F). Roll out pizza dough on a floured surface to 30cm diameter.

2

Add Sauce & Cheese

✔ Low risk Low hazard. Keep surfaces clean.

Spread tomato sauce evenly, leaving 2cm border. Top with sliced mozzarella.

3

Bake Pizza

⛔ High risk Hot oven or hot tray. Use mitts.

Bake for 12-15 minutes until crust is golden and cheese is bubbly.

4

Finish & Serve

✔ Low risk Low hazard. Keep surfaces clean.

Remove from oven, top with fresh basil leaves, drizzle with olive oil, and slice.

Pepperoni Classic Pizza

Prep Time 25 mins
Cook Time 20 mins
Difficulty Easy
Serves

Safety notes

⛔ High risk Oven at 240°C. Mitigation: use oven mitts and a timer, keep children away.

⚠ Medium risk Contains gluten and dairy. Mitigation: check labels if you have allergies.

✔ Low risk Raw dough. Mitigation: wash hands and tools after handling.

Ingredients

  • 125g pizza dough
  • 250g pizza dough
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 750g pizza dough
  • 50ml tomato sauce
  • 100ml tomato sauce
  • 200ml tomato sauce
  • 300ml tomato sauce
  • 75g pepperoni
  • 150g pepperoni
  • 300g pepperoni
  • 450g pepperoni
  • 100g mozzarella cheese
  • 1 tsp oregano

Instructions

1

Prepare Base

⛔ High risk Hot oven or hot tray. Use mitts.

Preheat oven to 230°C (450°F). Roll out dough and place on pizza stone.

2

Add Toppings

✔ Low risk Low hazard. Keep surfaces clean.

Spread sauce, add cheese, and arrange pepperoni slices evenly.

3

Bake to Perfection

⛔ High risk Hot oven or hot tray. Use mitts.

Bake for 15-18 minutes until crust is crispy and cheese is golden.

4

Serve Hot

✔ Low risk Low hazard. Keep surfaces clean.

Sprinkle with oregano and slice while hot. Enjoy immediately!

🍕 Cook-Along: Margherita Pizza

Prologue

Every pizza starts with a story

Gather your ingredients and preheat the oven to 240°C (465°F). The kitchen is quiet, the flour is waiting, and tonight you are the baker.

⏱ Preparation: 5 minutes

⚠ Medium risk Contains gluten and dairy. Check labels for allergies.

Chapter 1

The Dough

Roll out your pizza dough on a floured surface to about 30 cm across. Be patient; good dough rewards a gentle hand.

⏱ Time: 5 minutes

✔ Low risk Wash your hands and keep the surface dry and floured.

Chapter 2

Sauce & Cheese

Spread the tomato sauce evenly, leaving a thin edge for the crust. Then scatter the mozzarella and let the colors of Italy appear.

⏱ Time: 3 minutes

⚠ Medium risk Dairy allergen. Wipe spills so the counter is not slippery.

Chapter 3

The Bake

Slide the pizza into the hot oven and bake for 12–15 minutes, until the crust is golden and the cheese bubbles.

⏱ Time: 15 minutes

⛔ High risk The oven is 240°C. Use oven mitts, keep children away, and set a timer.

Epilogue

Complete! 🎉

Your Margherita pizza is ready. Add fresh basil, let it rest for a minute, and enjoy the first slice.

Bricks and Stones Recipe Studio — Group Project

Course: CSC 4370/6370 — Web Programming — Prof. Henry Louis

Project Summary

Bricks and Stones Recipe Studio is a recipe website for pizza lovers, built with pure HTML and CSS (Track A: Recipe Studio, Story Pack extension). Key features include hover-activated card flips revealing nutritional information, category filtering for menu items, persistent shopping cart updates, and a complete order workflow—all achieved through innovative CSS-only state management.

Group Members

Kothoju Naresh (002923882), Sri Krishna Teja Gangina (002923905), and Sattwik Bhattacharjee (002960354)

How the AI-style features work

Everything here is a UI simulation in HTML and CSS. No model, no API, no JavaScript.

AI Assistant Panel
Conversational interface. Intent cards map a question to a pre-written response template.
Recommendation Engine UI
Recommender system with explainable AI. Fixed rules read your saved items and cart, then show a confidence meter and a "Why?" note.
Predictive Progress Dashboard
Forecasting. Milestone cards show estimated time to go and fill in as you complete them.
Computer Vision Overlay
Object detection. A scan line runs, then labeled bounding boxes appear with confidence scores.
AI Ethics & Safety Notices
Risk labeling and transparency. Low, Medium, and High badges come with a clear mitigation.
Model Comparison Cards
Model trade-offs. Speed, quality, and cost bars let you choose a suggestion model.

⛔ High risk High: stop and use the mitigation first.
